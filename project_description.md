# CSU IS Career Hub Project Description

## Purpose
CSU IS Career Hub is a student-focused internship, co-op, and early-career career platform built for Cleveland State University Information Systems students. Its purpose is to help students not only find relevant opportunities, but also understand how to become more qualified for them. Instead of functioning as a generic job board, the platform connects job discovery with employer research, certifications, student reviews, alumni insight, and application tracking.

The project is designed to support students at multiple stages of career preparation:
- exploring employers and roles
- identifying skill and certification gaps
- tracking progress toward qualifications
- applying to relevant opportunities
- learning from other students and alumni

## Core Features

### 1. Job Board for Internships, Co-ops, and Early-Career Roles
The platform lists active opportunities relevant to CSU students, including internships, co-ops, part-time roles, and entry-level jobs. Students can:
- browse current openings
- filter by role, experience level, and location
- save jobs for later
- view job details such as company, pay range, and certifications tied to the role
- apply directly through CSU-hosted application flows when supported

### 2. Certification Discovery and Role Alignment
One of the platform’s central goals is helping students become more competitive candidates. The certifications section allows students to:
- explore certifications connected to different role tracks
- view related jobs tied to each certification
- save certifications
- mark certifications as interested, planned, in progress, or completed
- manage certification progress through a dedicated learning plan

### 3. Personalized Readiness and Skill-Gap Guidance
The platform includes recommendation and readiness features that connect student goals to actual job requirements. Based on saved jobs, target roles, preferred location, and certification progress, students can:
- view recommended certifications
- see top skill or qualification gaps
- understand which certifications are helping their fit
- track how close they are to the certifications listed for a role

### 4. Company Directory and Employer Profiles
Students can research employers beyond the job listing itself. Company features include:
- a directory of companies with active openings
- company detail pages
- role and hiring focus summaries
- review counts and ratings
- job openings by employer
- salary-related context where available

### 5. Student Reviews and Moderation
The review system allows students to contribute insight from internship and work experiences. Features include:
- internship and employer review submission
- published reviews on company pages
- review browsing across the platform
- admin moderation tools for approval and rejection

### 6. Alumni and Career Insight
The alumni section helps students understand real career paths from people connected to CSU. Students can:
- browse alumni profiles
- learn how alumni got into their current roles
- read experience highlights and advice
- save alumni profiles for later reference

### 7. Application and Progress Tracking
The application system supports more than simple submission. Students can:
- apply to CSU-hosted roles
- attach resumes and cover letters
- manage a default resume
- track application stages such as submitted, reviewing, interviewing, offer, rejected, and closed
- add follow-up dates and notes

### 8. Profile and Saved Items Management
Students can maintain a career-oriented profile that includes:
- target roles
- preferred location
- opportunity types
- bio and major
- saved jobs, certifications, companies, and alumni

## What Makes It Unique
What makes CSU IS Career Hub unique is that it is not just a place to search for jobs. It combines career exploration, qualification planning, and application management into one student-centered workflow.

Unlike a traditional job platform, it is built around the question:
"How do I become qualified for the roles I want?"

Its distinguishing strengths include:
- direct integration of job discovery with certification planning
- personalized readiness and gap analysis
- a learning plan tied to employability
- employer research with student reviews and alumni insight
- application tracking designed for students early in their careers
- a focus on CSU Information Systems students and their real local opportunity pipeline

This makes the platform both a job board and a career development tool.

## Summary
CSU IS Career Hub is a specialized career platform for CSU Information Systems students that combines job search, certification discovery, company research, student reviews, alumni insight, and progress tracking. Its strongest value is that it helps students move from simply browsing opportunities to actively building the qualifications needed to pursue them successfully.
